## Failure scenario

A campaign offers messages but never sees a complete projection declaration.
Another uses only a fake embedding engine, requests recovery that stays Disabled,
or terminates only at one of several required crash boundaries. Every observed
safety condition can hold while the intended acceptance situations are absent.
A metric reporting some marker hits lets these omissions look successful.

A competing explanation is genuinely unrelated optional scope. That is allowed
only when declared before the campaign. It cannot remove a required supported
RP2.1 path, a five-class requirement or an inconvenient failed scenario. Unknown
conditional applicability is not an exclusion. Disabled external optional lanes
remain disabled and do not count as successful required-path exercise.

## Timing windows and dependencies

Before execution, freeze nonempty finite matrix `R` from all three maps and
approved scope. A cell is `(constant marker, scenario_id)` with bounded declared
scenario values: source class, harness, hook/entry point, gate reason, identity
dimension, recovery mode and crash boundary where applicable.
During execution, retain independent event observations keyed to those cells.
At completed-campaign finalization, evaluate `sometimes` on the conjunction that
approval/scope is valid and every required cell has a known observed witness.
The root marker is not included in its own prerequisite matrix.
Unknown, skipped and unfired required cells make the conjunction false.
The check does not require safety violations; correctness is judged separately.
All numeric limits, recovery windows and work caps remain RP2.9 approvals.

## What a test must construct

1. An approved immutable campaign manifest binding the three map revisions and
   the required marker/scenario cells, plus independently justified exclusions.
2. Witnesses for all five source classes and actual completeness declarations
   over nonempty independent inventories, not merely offered inputs.
3. Offered input observed at the required certified inference path, with
   certification/artifact identity and exact input witness from its owner.
4. External barrier, termination and reopened-state observations for every
   declared local, ack, embedding-persistence and selector crash boundary.
5. Actual `Current` recovery state for admitted normal catch-up and authorized
   recovery modes, plus required rebuild/embedding outcomes under approved bounds.
6. Separate missing, failed and unsupported/inapplicable evidence cells, and
   required supported-path controls that the negative cases cannot substitute for.
7. A completed witness ledger whose missing/unknown/skipped cells cause failure.
   It must not silently rebuild `R` from the markers that happened to fire.
8. Fixed root marker `rp21_projection_acceptance_situations_witnessed`; all
   scenario identities are data attached to constant marker names.

The [binding matrix](../fault-map.md#binding-acceptance-matrix) is the reusable
handoff. No test form is selected here; `/testing:test-strategy` receives the
required observations and the coordinator owns approval and scope.
