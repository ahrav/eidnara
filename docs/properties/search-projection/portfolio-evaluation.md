## Binding coordinator decisions

**B1/B2:** Required situation coverage is an acceptance obligation, not a metric
or optional tally. The campaign owner freezes a nonempty marker/scenario matrix
before execution. Every required cell needs an independent witness, including
all five classes, nonempty completeness declaration, certified inference,
actual crash cuts, and required Current outcomes. Missing, skipped, unknown, or
unfired cells fail. The aggregate marker is not a prerequisite cell of itself.
Clearly unrelated optional checks may be excluded by declared scope, not by
post-run convenience. Unsupported optional lanes stay disabled and cannot
replace required supported-path witnesses. Unresolved conditional applicability
is not an implicit exemption.

**B4:** Older catalogs supply leads and reusable invariants, not inherited
exercise or reachability claims. Current production Claude tokenizer use and
Synapse composition contradict older absence claims. Certified inference is
explicit-config-only; the certified-runtime test's ignored annotation is not a
pass. Job eviction considers last poll with completion fallback. Host generation
staging has a CLI caller. Deleted mirror/effect subjects do not become current
work. The durable correction sections in the three inventories own these
qualifications; no older catalog is silently carried forward as current proof.

The testing owner's nine-group strategy is adopted. Conditional remediation
fits T3/T4, normal and authorized recovery fit T9, and acceptance situation
coverage spans all groups. The coordinator does not defer the whole crash
battery to a weekly run. Every future PR includes its applicable focused crash
regression; broader scheduled sampling supplements rather than replaces it.
